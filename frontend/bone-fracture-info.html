Bone Fractures

Comprehensive information about bone fractures, types, diagnosis, and recovery.

Overview

A bone fracture is a medical condition where there is a partial or complete break in the continuity of a bone. Proper diagnosis and treatment are essential for healing and preventing complications.

Symptoms

  • Severe pain at injury site
  • Swelling and bruising
  • Deformity or abnormal positioning
  • Inability to bear weight
  • Limited range of motion
  • Numbness or tingling

Causes & Risk Factors

  • Trauma from accidents
  • Sports injuries
  • Falls
  • Osteoporosis
  • Repetitive stress
  • Pathological conditions

Treatment Options

  • Immobilization with cast/splint
  • Surgical repair (if needed)
  • Pain management
  • Physical therapy
  • Bone healing supplements
  • Activity modification
