Diabetic Retinopathy

Comprehensive information about diabetic retinopathy, screening, and treatment options.

Overview

Diabetic retinopathy is a diabetes complication that affects eyes, caused by damage to blood vessels in the retina. It can lead to blindness if untreated, making regular screening essential for people with diabetes.

Symptoms

  • Blurred or fluctuating vision
  • Dark or empty areas in vision
  • Vision loss
  • Difficulty seeing colors
  • Floaters
  • Night vision problems

Causes & Risk Factors

  • High blood sugar levels
  • High blood pressure
  • High cholesterol
  • Duration of diabetes
  • Poor blood sugar control
  • Pregnancy

Treatment Options

  • Blood sugar control
  • Anti-VEGF injections
  • Laser treatment
  • Vitrectomy surgery
  • Blood pressure management
  • Regular eye exams
