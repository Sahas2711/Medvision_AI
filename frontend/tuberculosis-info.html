Tuberculosis (TB)

Comprehensive information about tuberculosis, symptoms, transmission, and treatment options.

Overview

Tuberculosis is a potentially serious infectious disease that mainly affects the lungs. It's caused by bacteria and spreads through airborne droplets when people with active TB cough, sneeze, or spit.

Symptoms

  • Persistent cough (3+ weeks)
  • Coughing up blood
  • Chest pain
  • Fatigue and weakness
  • Weight loss
  • Night sweats and fever

Causes & Risk Factors

  • Mycobacterium tuberculosis bacteria
  • Airborne transmission
  • Weakened immune system
  • Close contact with infected person
  • Poor living conditions
  • HIV infection

Treatment Options

  • Anti-TB medications (6-9 months)
  • Directly Observed Therapy (DOT)
  • Isolation during infectious period
  • Nutritional support
  • Regular monitoring
  • Contact tracing
